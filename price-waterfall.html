How the math works

Your cost price is the shelf price minus the retailer’s margin. Allowances and any early-pay discount come off that invoice. The deductions reserve is a percentage of the invoice you expect to lose to fines and chargebacks. Commission comes off what you’re paid. Freight (if you deliver) and your landed cost come off last. What’s left is what you keep.

The floor works the same chain backward: the lowest cost price where what you keep still equals the margin you need. Every retailer names its allowances differently, so put each of yours in the closest line.
